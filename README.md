Enhanced SwinUNet with Optimized Modulated Deformable SRU Network for Lung Cancer Segmentation and Classification
- Lung cancer is considered as one of the leading causes of cancer-related mortality worldwide due to the late diagnosis and complex tumor appearance in medical imaging modalities like the CT scans. The accurate segmentation and classification of lung tumors is significant for the early detection, severity estimation and clinical decision-making. The conventional deep learning models designed for the disease diagnosis has the challenges like low contrast boundaries, noise interference, irregular tumor shapes and variability among the patients. To address the limitations, this research proposed the deep learning model for automated lung cancer segmentation and classification. The proposed model is designed with pre-processing, transformer-based segmentation and deformable recurrent classification to improve robustness and accuracy. Initially, the anisotropic diffusion filtering is employed to suppress noise by preserving the significant edge information. Then, the Integrated RegNet Swin-UNet (IRS-UNet) is proposed for tumor segmentation, wherein the integrated attention mechanism and RegNet based MLP is employed to improve the segmentation accuracy. Finally, the segmented tumor regions are classified using the proposed Modulated Deformable Simple Recurrent Network (MDSNet) that is designed with the modulated deformable convolution and Simple Recurrent Units (SRU) for capturing spatial and sequential dependencies. Then, the optimal training convergence and improved generalization is employed using the proposed Chaotic Raindrop Optimization (ChR) algorithm is employed for loss function optimization.
